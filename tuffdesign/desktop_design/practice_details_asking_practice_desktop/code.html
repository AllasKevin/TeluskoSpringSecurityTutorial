About this Practice

The "Asking Practice" is a core module in the Relational Architect curriculum. It focuses on the shift from directive leadership to generative inquiry. In this session, you will explore the cognitive biases that prevent us from asking truly open questions and practice the 'Three-Level Inquiry' framework.

Unlike traditional training, this session uses real-time simulation and feedback loops to ensure that the skills are not just understood, but embodied. Prepare to be challenged and to unlearn the habit of "fixing" before you have truly "understood."

Session Objectives

check_circle

Identify and neutralize 'Leading Questions' that bias team outcomes.

check_circle

Master the '5-Second Silence' technique to encourage participation.

check_circle

Develop a personal library of 10 'Powerful Openers' for high-stakes meetings.

check_circle

Construct a feedback loop to measure your questioning-to-statement ratio.
